Downloaded October 30, 2026. Maps are bundled locally. Orbit projects the maps onto spheres, composites Earth's clouds and adds illustrative lighting and animation. The author describes these maps as based on NASA imagery/elevation with tuned colors and some fictional terrain filling gaps. Device worlds are symbolic; they are not real locations or astronomical scale models.
